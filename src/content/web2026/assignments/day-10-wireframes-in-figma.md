# Wireframes and Stylescapes

Finish what you started in class today!

1. **Mid-fi means**: real text hierarchy (big, medium, small), gray boxes for images, your actual navigation labels from Day 8. No colors, no fonts, no styling seductions; your design tokens handle those, and they are already in your `global.css`.
2. **Hierarchy first.** Before you draw, decide: what is the one thing each page most wants a visitor to see? Give it the space that says so.
3. **Two pages**: your homepage, plus whichever of your three sections feels most alive.


## Submitting

Post a screenshot (or Figma share link) in your dev note, with one sentence: *"The first thing I want a visitor to see is ___."
